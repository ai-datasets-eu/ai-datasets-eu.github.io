GenAI Surveys in Europe

This repository (EC AI) collects the surveys on generative artificial intelligence run in European countries.
To add a new survey, please make a request through GitHub.

Surveys fielded in 2026 put the share using AI at:

  • 54 to 61% of people
  • 31 to 52% of workers
  • 42 to 67% of firms

These ranges are wide, because each survey makes different choices when defining:

  • artificial intelligence tools
  • adoption and intensity
  • the target population
  • …

AI use by individuals

General population surveys ask whether people use AI for any purpose: at work, in their studies or in private life.

AI use by workers

Worker surveys ask whether people use AI in their job. Results depend on who is covered and how AI is named.

AI use by firms

Firm surveys ask whether the business uses AI. Results depend on firm size, weighting and what counts as use.
